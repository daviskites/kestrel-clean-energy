Government support

The economics may be better than you think.

Eligible homeowners in England and Wales can currently receive support through the Boiler Upgrade Scheme towards qualifying heat-pump installations.

Eligibility and grant values depend on property and technology. Kestrel would confirm suitability before any commitment.

£7,500

Current Boiler Upgrade Scheme support towards a qualifying air-source heat pump in England and Wales.

Some qualifying off-gas-grid oil/LPG properties can receive an additional £1,500 until March 2027.
